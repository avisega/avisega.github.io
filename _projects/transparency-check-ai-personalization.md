Transparency-Check is a practical framework developed under the European Horizon 2020 *WeNet - The Internet of Us* collaboration to make AI personalization systems more transparent, ethical, and user-centric. The project bridges ethical theory, regulation, and AI design by introducing a structured checklist that evaluates transparency across data input, algorithmic processing, output presentation, and user control. Applied to global platforms such as Amazon, Netflix, and Spotify, the framework exposes gaps in user trust and comprehension, translating them into actionable design principles for responsible AI development. Transparency-Check provides companies, researchers, and policymakers with a scalable instrument for auditing and improving algorithmic transparency—an essential capability in an era of regulatory scrutiny and growing demand for explainable, trustworthy AI.
