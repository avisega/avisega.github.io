This project introduces Trajectory-Corrected Intervention (TCI), a reinforcement learning framework for optimizing engagement policies in large-scale human-AI systems. TCI learns when and how to intervene by balancing short-term disruption costs with long-term behavioral outcomes. The method combines model-based policy optimization with offline evaluation, iteratively refining both the representation of user trajectories and the resulting decision policy without relying on online experimentation. Developed jointly with Microsoft Research and the University of Oxford, TCI was deployed in a live online platform and demonstrated significant improvements in sustained engagement and contribution. The project highlights how hybrid AI systems can safely and effectively optimize interventions in dynamic, human-centered environments.
